You're talking to an AI assistant that helps you explore Swoop's adventures. Here's what that means for your data, in plain English.

## What we keep

We store your messages and the assistant's replies for the length of your conversation, plus a short window after, so that a Swoop specialist can pick up the thread if you ask to be put in touch. We don't store anything else about you unless you choose to share contact details at the handoff step.

## How long we keep it

If you don't share contact details, the conversation is held for a short retention window and then deleted. If you do share contact details and ask Swoop to follow up, the record is kept for up to twelve months, or until your enquiry is moved into Swoop's CRM, whichever comes first. Conversations that never reach a handoff are deleted on the schedule set out in our retention summary.

## Who processes your data

To run the assistant, Swoop relies on two technical suppliers:

- **Anthropic** — provides the AI model that generates the assistant's replies. Your messages pass through their API to produce a response. Anthropic's terms for this use prohibit training their models on your conversation.
- **Google Cloud Platform** — hosts the assistant's infrastructure and short-term conversation storage in the EU region.

Both have data-processing agreements in place with Swoop covering this use.

## Your rights

You can ask us to delete your conversation, your contact details, or both, at any time. Email **privacy@swoopadventures.co** and tell us roughly when you spoke to the assistant and the email address you gave (if any), and we'll find and delete the record within a reasonable window.

You can also ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct anything that's wrong. Same address.

## Questions

If anything here isn't clear, or you want to know more about how Swoop handles personal data generally, see our main privacy policy at swoopadventures.co/privacy or email **privacy@swoopadventures.co**.
