# Evalset growth log

Append-only record of weekly evalset-growth ritual sessions. One line per session. See `evalset-growth.md` for the ritual itself.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD | sessions reviewed | scenarios authored | issues raised | notes`

- `sessions reviewed`: how many transcripts were triaged in Step 1.
- `scenarios authored`: how many new YAML files landed under `product/harness/scenarios/`.
- `issues raised`: how many separate bugs / content tickets were filed.
- `notes`: optional, one short clause. Useful for skipped weeks ("skipped, low traffic"), pattern observations ("3rd voice scenario this month"), or links to escalation entries in `inbox.md`.
