Use this when the visitor asks a concrete factual question and expects a concrete answer. "How long is the W trek?" "Is December really that crowded?" "Do I need a visa?" "What's the weather like in March?" "How do I get from Punta Arenas to the park?" These are the workhorse questions. The agent's job here is to be useful and specific, not to weave atmosphere.

The output is the most relevant prose chunks from FAQ, practical-guide pages, month-by-month content, and trip-detail prose. Each chunk includes a canonical URL. Offer it when the visitor would benefit from reading the full page, especially for anything where the detail matters more than the summary (visa rules, transport logistics, packing lists). Quote directly when the source is precise. Paraphrase when the source is verbose.

*When to pick this:* the visitor wants a specific answer to a specific question. If they're exploring a feeling rather than asking a fact, that's `find_inspiring`. If they're asking *which trip*, that's `find_options`. `lookup` is for the practical, the procedural, the "I just need to know X". `lookup` is for the questions where being a librarian for a moment is the friendliest thing the agent can be.
