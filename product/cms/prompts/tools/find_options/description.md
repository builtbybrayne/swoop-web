Use this when the visitor is ready to consider concrete trips. They've moved past "tell me about Patagonia" and into "what would I actually do?", usually with a region in mind, sometimes a duration, often a sense of the budget bracket they're working in. Construct the filter from what they've said in the conversation; don't make them recite preferences they've already shared.

The output is two to four trip cards: image, headline, one-line vibe pitch, region, headline price, duration, and a canonical URL. Frame each one — what's distinctive, why it matches what they've shared. Headline pricing is always "from £X"; never quote a definitive total or imply availability for a specific date. If the visitor pushes for those, that's the moment to suggest a specialist conversation, which is what `handoff` is for.

*When to pick this:* the visitor wants to look at concrete trips. If their interest is still abstract, `find_inspiring` is the right move; offering trip cards too early can feel like a hard sell. If they want to look hard at one specific trip's logistics, `lookup` will surface the practical detail. `find_options` is the closest the agent gets to recommending; use it when the conversation has earned that move.
