Use this when prose alone is doing too much work and a picture would carry more of the load. The visitor mentioned glaciers — show them a glacier. They asked what the W trek looks like — show them the trail. They said "somewhere with no one else around" — show them a refugio at last light, no one in frame. Imagery isn't decoration here; it's part of how Patagonia gets across, because the place is more felt than described.

The input is keywords (and optionally a region slug). The output is curated images: id, URL, alt text, optional caption. Hand them to the widget, which renders them inline — the agent doesn't narrate the picture, it lets the picture do its work. Two to four images is usually right; more becomes a slideshow and breaks the rhythm of the conversation.

*When to pick this:* the visitor's energy is visual or sensory, or you've reached for a content tool (`find_inspiring`, `find_someone_who`, `find_options`) and want to amplify what it returned with the matching image. Skip when the visitor's question is concrete and factual — `lookup` covers that without needing imagery. `illustrate` is the visual companion to any of the other tools; it doesn't drive the conversation, it lifts it.
